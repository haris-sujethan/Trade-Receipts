# Home page

Scope: the public home page (first visit, before sign-up and the IDE). Mode: Persuade.

Audience: anyone who trades or invests, coder or not; hackathon judges arriving cold. Job: understand that they can describe a strategy in plain English and get honest, tested results and code they keep. Action: type a strategy into the working input and launch the IDE (or press Launch IDE). Proof: the product itself at work, with example values labeled as examples. No testimonials, user counts, or benchmarks exist; none may be invented.

Sections (confirmed): first viewport; product window (the IDE in a framed card with example tabs that replay like a short video); how it works (three steps). Footer: data credits ("Data from Tiingo," "Crypto data: Binance Vision") and "Not financial advice." More sections may be added later.

Constraints: no guru or copy-trading framing; no competitor comparisons; no Meta logo; the Trade Receipts wordmark comes from one constant. Avoid: flashy, toy-like, too dense, generic AI look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every strategy ships with a facts label: what it claimed beside what it actually did. Refuses the category default of a dark fintech hero with glowing charts, and the headline-over-screenshot template.

OWN-WORLD: The FDA Nutrition Facts panel as a working system. White stock, near-black ink, heavy black rules in three weights (hairline, bar, slab), one grotesque from Regular to Black with tabular figures, rows set name-left value-right. One red only for contradicted, failed, or trailing values. Square corners; no shadows, gradients, pills, or glows.

STORY: The visitor sees a claimed number struck through beside the tested one, believes this tool tells the truth about strategies (and stocks, from filings), and types their own strategy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav: wordmark left, two links, black Launch IDE button right, slab rule beneath. Left column: Black-weight headline about 96px, one subline, the working input (thick black frame, black Test it button), three example prompts. Right column: a full-scale Strategy Facts label with example values and its footnote. Primary action is Test it.

FORM: Strategy Facts Label (FDA Nutrition Facts panel), #1 on my ordered list, chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled #6. seed:474b68b6cb7c6351. Signature interaction: the Claimed row strikes through and the Tested value settles in; the product window's tabs replay agent steps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
